**A parallel example (not your source):** Imagine a school's detention book for one month. Just names, dates, and reasons. Even so, it shows:
- which rules the school bothered to enforce (tardiness? talking? uniform?)
- who got caught, and whether the same few names keep recurring
- how the penalties compare across offences

It can't show who broke rules without being noticed, which teachers let things slide, or what students thought of the punishments. And if the book goes blank for a week, you'd want to know why. Maybe nobody misbehaved, or maybe school was closed.
