Here's a parallel example on invented material, not your excerpt:

> *A mill owner's notice: "The weavers were stirred up by a stranger from the city. He was seen at the mill gate on Tuesday, and the looms stopped at noon."*

- **Claim:** the weavers were stirred up by a stranger. That's the owner's conclusion, stated as fact.
