**What the task is asking**

Reader unit 3 gives you a sequence:

1. **Author's position and interest:** Who wrote it, to whom, and what did they want?
2. **What they could see vs. what was out of view:** What did their position let them observe, and what couldn't they know?
3. **Word choices:** Where did they pick one word when another was available?
4. **Can show / cannot show:** What does the document reveal (even unintentionally), and what can't it tell us?

Your 150 words are basically that two-column list written as prose.

**A worked example on parallel material (invented)**

Imagine a letter from a ship's captain to the owners: *"The crew refused duty on Tuesday. I believe most were misled by the cook and two seamen from another vessel. I have kept the ship secure and await instructions."*

- **Position and interest:** The captain is answering to the owners and wants to look in control, so he has reason to present the refusal as the work of a few troublemakers.
- **Could see / out of view:** He saw the deck and the refusal itself. He could not see private conversations in the forecastle or the crew's reasons.
- **Word choices:** "Refused duty" and "misled" cast the crew as disobedient and easily led, not as people with grievances.
- **Can show:** The captain's version of events, the language of authority, and which crew members he bothered to name or identify.
- **Cannot show:** What the crew wanted, or whether "misled" is true.

Notice that I made a claim about the author's interest and then tied it to specific wording. That's the move your worksheet needs.
