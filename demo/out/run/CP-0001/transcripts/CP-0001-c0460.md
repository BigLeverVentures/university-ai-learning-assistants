Here's a parallel example on made-up material, not your page. Imagine a school detention book:

> *Mon: Chen, late, 1 detention. Mon: Osei, phone in class, 2 detentions. Wed: Chen, late, 1 detention. Fri: [no entries].*

- **What it shows about discipline:** lateness and phones were punishable offences, there was a scale of penalties, and Chen turns up more than once.
