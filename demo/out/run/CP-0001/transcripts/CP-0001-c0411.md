**What the task is asking (from Reader unit 3):** Every document was made by someone, for someone, to do something. Evaluating a source means writing a two-column list in prose:
1. **Position and interest:** who wrote it, to whom, and what they wanted their reader to believe or do.

3. **Word choices:** places where the author picked one word when others were available.
4. **What the source can show** (the author's view, the vocabulary of authority, the names recorded) **and what it cannot** (the motives of the people described, events where the author wasn't present).
