**The sorting, from the Unit 2 reader**
- **Claim:** what the source asserts, in its own voice.
- **Evidence:** something reported as seen or happening, which you could check against other sources, like a date, a name, or an event.
- **Inference:** the step *you* take from evidence toward a claim. It belongs to you, not the document.

**Applied to your sentence:** "Misled by outside agitators" is a claim. The editorial states it as fact, but it's the conclusion the editor wants readers to accept. "Outside agitators caused the strike" is the same claim reworded, so it can't also be the evidence. That's the mix-up the reader warns about.

Notice that the evidence is a thing someone could have observed, and the inference is the gap between that observation and the claim.
