**A parallel example (not from your packet):** Imagine a factory owner writing to his board of directors about a walkout, and a worker writing years later to a local newspaper about the same walkout.

- The owner knows the board will judge him on whether he kept the plant under control. He has a reason to describe the situation as orderly and the strikers as few, or as led by outsiders.
- The worker knows the public may remember the strike as reckless. He has a reason to describe the strikers as disciplined and the decision as the workers' own.

Each could be sincere, and each could be accurate about what he personally saw. But each is also making a case. The audience tells you what the case is, and where to look for things that are inference rather than observation.
