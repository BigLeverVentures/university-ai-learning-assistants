**Step 1: Provenance (who, for whom, what interest).**
The writer is the owner, writing privately to a partner. He has money at stake and wants to appear in control. That shapes what he says.

**Step 2: What he could see and what was out of view.**

**Step 3: Word choices.**
"Agitators" rather than "organizers" or "spokesmen." "Misled" suggests the workers couldn't have decided for themselves. These choices show his view, and the vocabulary of someone in authority.

**Step 4: Can show / cannot show.**
- *Can show:* how an owner framed a strike to a partner, and the names or groups he noticed.
- *Cannot show:* why the workers actually left, or whether "most" of them really wanted to return. That is his claim, not evidence.
