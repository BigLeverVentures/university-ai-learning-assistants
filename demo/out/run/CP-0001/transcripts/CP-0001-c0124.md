**The three categories (from the unit 2 reader):**
- **Claim:** what the source asserts. It's stated in the author's voice, often as if it were plain fact.
- **Evidence:** something the source reports that you could check against other sources, like a time, a place, or something someone saw.
- **Inference:** the step *you* take from evidence toward a claim. It belongs to you, not the document.

**Practice example (invented, not your material):** Suppose a town paper says, "The bakery failed because the owner was careless with money. Its lights were on past midnight three nights running, and a supplier was seen leaving its door."

- Claim: "the owner was careless with money."
- Evidence: the lights on past midnight and the supplier seen leaving. Those are things someone could observe and check.
- Inference (the reader's): maybe the owner was struggling with the books, or maybe a late order was being baked. The evidence doesn't decide between them.
