Here is why audience matters in general. People shape an account around what the reader will do with it. A parallel example: a student explaining a missed deadline to a professor will stress the things that make the lateness understandable, and a message to a friend about the same week would emphasize different things. Neither has to be a lie, but each selects and frames.

Audience also affects what a writer can safely say. A foreman reporting to a superintendent has reasons to show he stayed at his post. A man writing in 1902 about a book that contradicts him has reasons to correct the record.
