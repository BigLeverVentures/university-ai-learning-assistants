**Invented source:** A harbor master writes to the town council in 1890: "The fishermen have *refused* to land their catch at the new dock. Two *agitators* from the cannery have been *stirring them up*. I believe most would comply if the fee were waived, but I cannot say so officially."

- **Position and interest:** He's an official enforcing a council rule, writing to the people who employ him. He wants to look in control and not to be blamed.

- **Word choice:** "Agitators" and "stirring up" suggest the fishermen were led rather than acting on their own reasons. "Refused" frames it as defiance, where "did not land" would be neutral.
