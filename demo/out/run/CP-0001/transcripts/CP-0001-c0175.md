Here's a worked example on a different, invented source, so you can see the moves.

*Source: a lighthouse keeper's report to the district inspector after a ship ran aground near his station.*

- **Position and interest:** The keeper is an employee writing to his supervisor, and his job may be at risk if the lamp is blamed. So he wants to appear careful.
- **Could see / out of view:** He could see his lamp, the weather, and his own log. He could not see what the ship's crew saw or decided.
- **Word choices:** He writes the lamp "was extinguished by a squall." He could have written "went out" or "failed." His wording shifts responsibility to the weather.
- **Can show:** How a keeper explained himself to an inspector, what he thought counted as a good excuse, and what he chose to record.
- **Cannot show:** Whether the lamp was actually well maintained, or why the ship ran aground.

Notice that the "can't show" column is not "he might be lying." It's about what his position and purpose put out of reach.
