**Example: a school detention book**
- *What it was for:* a teacher records date, pupil, offence, and penalty so the school can enforce its rules consistently.
- *What it shows about discipline:* which rules the school bothered to enforce (uniform, lateness, talking?), who got caught, and how often. If one pupil appears six times and others once, that's a pattern in enforcement.
- *What it's silent about:* misbehavior nobody noticed, offences a teacher let slide, and what pupils thought of the punishments.
- *Across time:* if entries stop for a week, that doesn't mean perfect behavior. It might mean the school was closed. A gap is a fact to be explained.

Notice that the three categories (purpose, what it shows, what it's silent about) stay separate. That's what the unit says a good analysis does.
