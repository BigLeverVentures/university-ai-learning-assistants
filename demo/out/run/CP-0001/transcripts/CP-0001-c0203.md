**The idea (from Reader unit 3):** Every document was made by someone, for someone, to do something. Evaluating a source means building a two-column list, then writing it in prose:

- **What it can show:** the author's view, the vocabulary of authority, and the names the author recorded.
- **What it cannot show:** the motives of the people described, and anything that happened where the author wasn't looking.

To fill in those columns, you work in steps: name the author's position and interest, ask what they could see and what was out of view, and notice word choices where other words were available.

- *Cannot show:* why the workers actually left, since he's guessing at their motives.
