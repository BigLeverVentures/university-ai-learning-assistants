Here's a parallel example on made-up material, not your excerpt. Imagine a school detention book for one week in March:

- *Mon: Lee, late, 10 min. Tue: Park, talking, 5 min. Wed: Lee, late, 10 min. Thu: Lee, late, 10 min. Fri: [no entries].*
- **Down the page:** Lee appears three times, and every charge but one is lateness. So the book shows the school enforced punctuality, and that one student was caught repeatedly.

- **What it can't show:** who misbehaved unnoticed, which offences were let off, or what Lee thought of the rule.
