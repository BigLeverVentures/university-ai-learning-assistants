**The method (Reader unit 3)**

**Practice example (invented, not your assignment)**
Imagine a mill owner's letter to a newspaper: *"A few agitators misled our loyal hands into abandoning their looms."*
- **Position and interest:** The owner is writing to the public and wants to look reasonable and in control.
- **Could see and couldn't see:** He could see who stopped work and when. He couldn't see what was said at the workers' meetings or why individuals joined.
- **Word choice:** "Agitators" and "misled" frame the workers as followers, not people with grievances of their own.
- **Can show / cannot show:** It can show how an owner wanted the strike understood and the vocabulary of authority. It cannot show the workers' motives.

Notice that each point rests on specific words, and that the "cannot show" side is as important as the "can show" side. Also, the owner's claim about "loyal hands" is a claim to check, not a fact.
