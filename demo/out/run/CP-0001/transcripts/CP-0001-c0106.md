**The core idea:** every document was made by someone, for someone, to do something. Evaluating a source means building a two-column list, written as prose:

- **What the document can show:** the author's view, the vocabulary of authority, the names that were recorded.
- **What it cannot show:** the motives of the people described, and anything that happened where the author wasn't.
