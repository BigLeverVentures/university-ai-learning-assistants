**What the task asks (Reader unit 3):** You're not summarizing the letter. You're answering three things:
1. **Whose perspective is this?** Name the author's position and interest: who is writing, to whom, and what they want.
2. **What can it show?** This includes what the author says, but also what the document reveals without meaning to, such as word choices, names recorded, and what the author thought the reader wanted to hear.
3. **What can't it show?** What was out of the author's view, and whose motives or actions he can't speak for.

**Worked example (invented, not your source):** A town clerk writes to the mayor in 1890 about a cannery fire: "The fire was started by idle boys who loiter by the docks. I was at the council hall until midnight and arrived to find the building lost."

- *Perspective:* A clerk reporting to his superior, likely wanting to avoid blame for the town's response.
- *Can show:* How an official explained a disaster. "Idle boys" is a loaded choice over, say, "unknown persons," so it shows who authorities were ready to blame. It also shows the clerk was not present when the fire began.
- *Can't show:* What caused the fire, or what the boys were actually doing, since he never saw it and doesn't name anyone.

Notice the last point follows from the first two: his position explains what he could and couldn't see.
