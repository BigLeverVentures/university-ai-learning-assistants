**The method in brief**
1. **Name the author's position and interest.** Who wrote it, to whom, and what did they want the reader to believe or do?

3. **Notice word choices** where other words were available.

**A parallel example (not your source)**
Imagine a mill owner's letter to a newspaper about a walkout, saying "agitators" pulled the workers off the looms.
- *Position and interest:* the owner wants the public to see him as reasonable and the workers as misled.

- *Word choice:* "agitators" instead of "organizers" or "representatives."
- *Can show:* how owners described strikers, and what the owner wanted the public to believe. *Cannot show:* why the workers actually left.
