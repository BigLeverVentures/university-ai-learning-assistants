**A parallel example (not your source).** Imagine a school's detention book: date, pupil, teacher, reason, minutes. Without any narrative, you could still see:

- **What rules were enforced.** If "chewing gum" and "late" appear but "unkind words" never does, the school policed some behavior and not other behavior.
