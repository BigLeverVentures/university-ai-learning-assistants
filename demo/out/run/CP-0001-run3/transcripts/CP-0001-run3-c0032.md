*Imagine a report from a lighthouse keeper to a district inspector after a shipwreck. The keeper writes that the schooner's crew "ignored the warning light and were plainly careless," and that he "kept the lamp burning all night."*

- **Position and interest:** The keeper could be blamed if the light failed, so he wants to show he did his job.

- **Word choice:** "Ignored" and "careless" assign blame, where "did not turn in time" would only describe events.
- **Can show:** How a keeper explained himself to an inspector, and what he thought counted as doing his duty. **Cannot show:** why the crew acted as they did.
