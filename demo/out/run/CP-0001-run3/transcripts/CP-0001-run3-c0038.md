**Parallel example (not your assignment):**
*A schoolteacher writes to the school board in 1893: "Attendance has fallen because the farmers are careless of their children's learning. Only the Ostrander boy has shown real application. I trust the board will see that my salary is not reduced."*

1. **Author's position and interest:** The teacher is employed by the board and worried about her pay. She is writing to look competent and to blame someone else.
2. **What she could see, and what was out of view:** She saw who came to class and who didn't. She did not see the farms, the harvest work, or the families' reasons.
3. **Word choices:** "Careless" is a judgment, where "absent" or "busy with harvest" would have been neutral.
4. **Can show:** the teacher's view, what she thought the board wanted to hear, and which child she bothered to name.
5. **Cannot show:** why the families kept children home, or what the farmers thought of the school.

The written entry would then turn those five points into connected prose, with the "shows" and "cannot show" parts each getting a sentence or two.
