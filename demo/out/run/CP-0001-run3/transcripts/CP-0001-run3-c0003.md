Suppose a lighthouse keeper writes to the harbor board after a ship ran aground, saying, "The captain ignored my signals and was plainly careless."

- *Word choice:* "Plainly careless" is a judgment, not an observation. "Ignored" assumes the captain saw the signals.
- *Can show:* The keeper's account, what he believed the board wanted to hear, and that signals were sent.
- *Cannot show:* The captain's reasons, or what happened on the ship.
