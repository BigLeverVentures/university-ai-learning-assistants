6. **assistant**: Yes, it matters a lot, and it's one of the main ideas in Unit 3. A writer shapes a document for the person who will read it, so audience affects what gets included, how it's framed, and what gets left out.

Here's a quick parallel. Imagine a ship's mate describes the same storm damage in three places:

- **In the official log for the captain:** "Cargo shifted despite proper stowage."
- **In a letter to his wife:** "I thought we would go down."
- **In testimony to an insurance inquiry:** "Weather exceeded anything reasonably foreseeable."

Same storm, three different documents, because each reader wants something different and the mate wants something different from each. If you only had the log, you'd learn how a mate talks to his captain, but not what the storm was like.
