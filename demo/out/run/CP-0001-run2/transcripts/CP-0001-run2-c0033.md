**What "perspective" means here:** every document is written by a particular person, to a particular reader, for a reason. That affects what the document tells you and what it leaves out.

**A parallel example (not your source):** Imagine a restaurant manager emails the owner after the kitchen staff walk out. She writes, "A few troublemakers stirred everyone up. Most of my cooks wanted to stay." Ask yourself:
- Who is she writing to, and what does she want that person to think? Probably that she's in control and not to blame.
- What could she see? The walkout itself, and who was in the room.
- What couldn't she see? Why the cooks were really angry, since she's describing them rather than speaking for them.

So her email is good evidence of how a manager explained a walkout to her boss. It's weak evidence of what the cooks wanted.
