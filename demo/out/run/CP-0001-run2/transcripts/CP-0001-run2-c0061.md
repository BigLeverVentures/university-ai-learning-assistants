**The idea:** every document was written by someone, for someone, to get something done. To evaluate a source, you ask what that position lets the document show, and what it hides.

**A made-up example, not a course source:** a student emails a professor: *"Professor, I'm sorry this is late. My printer broke and I'll have it to you tonight."*

- **Who and to whom?** A student writing to the person who grades them.
- **What do they want?** For the professor to accept the lateness without a penalty.
- **What can this email show?** What excuse the student chose to offer, and that they see the professor as someone to apologize to.
- **What can't it show?** Whether the printer really broke, or what the student was doing all week.

That's the whole method: author and audience, what they want, what the document shows, what it can't.
