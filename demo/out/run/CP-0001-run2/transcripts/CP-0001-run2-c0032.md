**A parallel example (not your source).** Say a shopkeeper writes to her landlord: *"Business has been terrible. Only a few customers come in, and the street is full of noisy children who scare people off. Please lower my rent."*

- **Perspective:** a shopkeeper writing to the person she owes rent to, and she wants something from him.
- **Can show:** what she wants the landlord to believe (that business is bad), and what she chose to blame (the children).
- **Cannot show:** whether business is really bad, or what the children or customers would say. She may be exaggerating because she wants lower rent.

The key idea is that a letter written to get something done tells you what the writer *wanted the reader to think*, not necessarily what happened.
