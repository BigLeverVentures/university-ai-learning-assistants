**Practice example (invented, not your assignment):** Imagine a mill owner's statement to a newspaper: *"A handful of agitators have forced our loyal hands from their looms. Production continues under the care of trusted staff."*

- **Position and interest:** The owner is writing for the public and wants to look in control and the workers to look misled.
- **Could see / couldn't see:** He could see the mill's output and his own managers. He couldn't see what was said in workers' meetings or why individuals walked out.
- **Word choices:** "Agitators" instead of "organizers," and "forced" instead of "persuaded," both imply the workers didn't act on their own.
- **Can show:** The owner's version of events, the language of authority, and what he wanted the public to believe.
- **Cannot show:** The workers' real motives or how many supported the walkout.

Notice that the statement tells us something even where it isn't trustworthy: it shows what the owner hoped to get away with saying.
