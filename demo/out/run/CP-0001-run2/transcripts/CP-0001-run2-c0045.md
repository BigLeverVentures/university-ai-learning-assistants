**What the entry asks, following Reader unit 3**

Every document was made by someone, for someone, to do something. So an evaluation has two columns, written as prose:

- **Perspective:** Who wrote this, from what position, and what did they want? What could they see from where they stood, and what was out of view?
- **Can show / cannot show:** What does the document reveal, even without meaning to (the author's view, the vocabulary of authority, the names recorded)? What can't it tell us (the motives of the people described, what happened where the author wasn't)?

The unit also suggests noticing word choices where other words were available. For example, "ringleaders" instead of "spokesmen."
