Here's a parallel example, on a made-up source. Say a mill owner writes to the town newspaper after a walkout: *"A handful of agitators pulled our loyal hands from their looms, and production was halted."*

- **Position and interest:** he owns the mill and is writing to the public, so he wants sympathy and wants the strike to look like outsiders' work.
- **Could and couldn't see:** he can describe the mill's output and his own losses, but probably wasn't at the workers' meetings.
- **Word choices:** "agitators" rather than "organizers," and "loyal hands" rather than "workers."
- **Can show:** how an owner framed the strike publicly, and what he cared about.
- **Cannot show:** why the workers actually walked out, or whether they were "loyal."
