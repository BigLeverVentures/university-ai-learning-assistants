**What the task is asking.** Reader unit 3 says to evaluate a source by building a two-column list and writing it up as prose:

1. **Position and interest.** Who made it, for whom, and what did they want the reader to believe or do?
2. **Vantage point.** What could the author see from where they stood, and what was out of view?
3. **Word choices.** Where did the author pick one word when others were available? The unit's examples are "ringleaders" vs. "spokesmen" and "abandoned" vs. "left."
4. **Can show / cannot show.** The source can show the author's view, the vocabulary of authority, and the names that were recorded. It cannot show the motives of the people described, or what happened where the author wasn't.
