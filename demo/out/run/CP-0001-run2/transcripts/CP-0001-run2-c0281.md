**The method (from Reader unit 3)**

2. List what the author could see from that position and what was out of view.
3. Notice word choices where other words were available. The Reader's examples are "ringleaders" vs. "spokesmen" and "abandoned" vs. "left".

**Practice example (not your assignment)**
Imagine a mill owner's letter to his investors: "A few agitators have misled our loyal hands, who are anxious to return."

- *Position:* The owner is writing to people who control his money, so he has a reason to make the situation look manageable.
- *Could see / couldn't see:* He sees the mill's output and the gate. He doesn't see what the workers say to each other at home.
- *Word choice:* "Agitators" suggests outsiders, and "misled" suggests the workers had no reasons of their own.
- *Can show:* How an owner framed a strike for investors. *Cannot show:* Why the workers actually walked out, or whether most of them wanted to return.

Notice that the example makes a claim, points to specific words, and labels what is inference.
