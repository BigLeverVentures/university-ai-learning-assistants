**The method (Reader unit 3):**

**A practice example on a different, invented source (not your document):**
Imagine a mill owner's letter to his investors saying, "A few agitators have misled our loyal hands into walking out."

- *Position and interest:* He owns the mill and is writing to people who want reassurance about their money.
- *Could see:* his own losses, the empty mill, and the names of people he considered troublemakers. *Out of view:* what the workers said at their own meetings, and why they walked out.
- *Word choice:* "agitators" and "misled" instead of "organizers" or "chose."
- *Can show:* how an owner described the strike to investors and what he wanted them to believe. *Cannot show:* what the workers actually wanted or why.

Notice that I never decided whether the owner was right. I only asked what his position let him see and say.
