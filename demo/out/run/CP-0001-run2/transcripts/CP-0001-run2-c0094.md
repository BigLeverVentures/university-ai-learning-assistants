**The steps (Reader unit 3):**

**Parallel example (not your source):** Suppose a harbor pilot writes to the port board after a ship ran aground: "The vessel's master ignored my signals and steered wide of the channel. I had no part in the loss."

- **Position and interest:** The pilot is writing to the board that could blame him, so he wants to protect his reputation.
- **Could see / couldn't see:** He saw the signals from the shore. He couldn't see the master's reasons or what happened on the ship's deck.
- **Word choice:** "Ignored" assumes the master saw the signals and chose not to respond. "Missed" or "did not answer" would claim less.
- **Can show:** how the pilot wanted the board to see events, and what he considered blameworthy.
- **Cannot show:** why the master steered as he did, or whether the signals were visible.
