**A worked example on parallel material (not your ledger)**

Imagine an invented log from a bakery, March 1920, with entries like "Mar 3, Pell, oven crew, late, 10c" and "Mar 4, Pell, oven crew, late, 10c," and no entries at all for Mar 15-17.

- *Purpose:* the owner wanted a record of charges and amounts, probably for deducting from wages.
- *Shows:* lateness was a fineable offence with a fixed price. One worker appears repeatedly, so enforcement may have been uneven or focused on particular people. The fines are small, which tells you something about how the bakery handled lateness.
- *Silent:* it can't tell you who was late and never caught, whether the foreman forgave some people, or what Pell thought of the fines. The blank days could mean nobody was fined, but also that nobody was working. You'd need other evidence to decide.
