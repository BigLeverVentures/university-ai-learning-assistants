**A parallel example: a school detention book**

Imagine a page from a school's detention book: date, pupil's name, teacher, reason, minutes served.

- **What it was for:** The school kept it to track and enforce its rules. Nobody wrote it to tell a story, which is why it's useful.
- **What it shows:** Which rules the school cared about (say, "chewing gum" and "late"), who got caught, and how often. If one pupil appears six times and most appear once, that tells you something about how the rules were applied.
