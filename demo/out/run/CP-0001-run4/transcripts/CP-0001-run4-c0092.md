Here's a quick practice example on invented material, not your excerpt.

*A made-up bakery's "spoiled loaves" book, one week:*
*Mon, Cole, burnt, 2 loaves. Tue, Cole, burnt, 3 loaves. Wed, Ibbs, dropped, 1 loaf. Thu, Cole, burnt, 2 loaves. Fri-Sat, [no entries; note: "oven repair"].*

- **Down the page:** Cole appears three times, always for the same thing. That's evidence about who was *recorded* making errors, and it's an inference to say Cole was the worst baker. Maybe Cole worked the oven that was failing, or was watched more closely.
- **Across time:** The entries stop Friday and Saturday. The blank doesn't mean perfect baking. The note suggests the oven wasn't running, so there was nothing to spoil. The silence has an explanation.
- **What it can't show:** mistakes nobody noticed, loaves the owner let slide, or what Cole thought about it.

So a list can show what rules were enforced, who got caught, and how often, while staying silent on other things. The Reader asks you to keep those three apart.
