Here's a parallel example on made-up material, not your page. Imagine a school detention book:

- *Mar 3, J. Lowe, talking, 10 min. Mar 5, J. Lowe, late, 10 min. Mar 9, J. Lowe, talking, 20 min. Mar 4, P. Haas, chewing gum, 10 min.*
- **Down the page:** the same name keeps recurring, so the rules may be enforced unevenly, or one student is watched more closely. The offences are small, so the school cares about order and punctuality.
- **Across time:** if the entries stop for two weeks, that might mean perfect behavior, or that school was closed. You'd need to check.
- **What it can't show:** the students who talked and weren't caught, or what Lowe thought of the punishments.

So the "showing" comes from reading the list in two directions, then keeping separate what the record was for, what it shows, and what it's silent about.
