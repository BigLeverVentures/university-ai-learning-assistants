**Parallel example: a school detention book.** Imagine a page with a date, a pupil's name, an offence, and a penalty.
- *Down the page:* Which names recur? Which offences are listed, and which are never listed? That shows what rules the school enforced and who got caught under them. The same name five times suggests something different from five names once each.
- *Across time:* Where do entries thin out or stop? If the book is blank for a week, it might mean good behavior, but it might also mean the school was closed. That's a silence to explain, not an absence of information.
- *What it can't show:* Misbehavior the teacher never saw, offences let off with a warning, or what the pupils thought of the rules. The book records only what a particular teacher was required to write down.

So the list does show something, but only about what the record-keeper recorded, and part of the skill is saying where that stops.
