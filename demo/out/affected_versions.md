## Why three questions, not one

Discovery location, cause location, and affected versions are three different questions. Both fictional reports in this demonstration were DISCOVERED in H1 (comment tier, attributed to asm-H1-0001). For CP-0001 the CAUSE is located one tier up, in H (rule H-R03), and the AFFECTED VERSIONS are every assembled assistant that inherits that rule — H1 and H2, but not S1 (a different subject branch) and not the U-only baseline (which never had the rule). For CP-0002 the cause is located where it was discovered — a local H1 material reference — so the affected set is H1 alone, even though the symptom (fabricating unavailable material) is one the university-level retained test SC-U-003 already covers and passes elsewhere. The first finding justifies a change to shared guidance that must be tested on both courses; the second must stay local, and a harness that could only reason about the discovering course would get one of the two wrong.
