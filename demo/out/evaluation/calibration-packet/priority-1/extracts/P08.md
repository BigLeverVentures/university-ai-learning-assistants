He didn't see the loaders' private discussions or their reasons. - **Word choices:** "Agitators" rather than "speakers," and "misled" rather than "persuaded" or "convinced." - **Can show:** how a manager framed a walkout, the vocabulary of authority, and what he thought the board wanted to hear. **Cannot show:** why the loaders actually left, or whether most were really "misled." In your entry, those four moves would be written as connected prose, not a list. Now over to you with Pruitt's letter.
